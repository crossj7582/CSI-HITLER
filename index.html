April 9, 1940 — Denmark and Norway

Germany invaded Denmark and Norway. Denmark surrendered quickly, while Norway resisted longer. Germany sought to protect its strategic position in Scandinavia, including iron-ore shipments from Sweden and access to Norwegian coastal bases.

May 10, 1940 — Western Europe

Germany attacked the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, and France. German forces advanced through the Low Countries and the Ardennes, helping defeat France.

April 1941 — The Balkans

Germany and its allies invaded Yugoslavia and Greece.

June 22, 1941 — Operation Barbarossa

Germany launched a massive invasion of the Soviet Union, opening a major eastern front.
